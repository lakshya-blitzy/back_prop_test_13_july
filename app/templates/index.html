The directory artifact resolves with or without a trailing slash and is served its overview page, so either spelling round-trips. relpath Required. ArtifactSpec.relpath - the repository- relative path, for display only. exists Bool. modified_iso str or None. Millisecond-precision UTC ISO-8601 ending in a literal Z, e.g. 2022-09-07T13:37:26.297Z, which is the form the artifacts themselves carry. Used verbatim as the datetime attribute. modified_display str or None. The human-readable form, e.g. "07 Sep 2022, 15:39", matching how the generated report tree renders build dates.
