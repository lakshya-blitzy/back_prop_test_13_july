WHICH ARTIFACT, AND WHICH GENERATOR. This file belongs to the FIRST of the project's two HTML contracts, and the two are never collapsed into one. cucumber-java 7.2.3 (pom.xml, retained as historical reference) pulls html-formatter 17.0.0, which produced the single reports page - one document carrying no external asset reference of any kind.
