This service makes the artifacts of a test run retrievable and browsable over HTTP. The suite generates JSON, HTML and Txt reports as well as a rendered PrettyReports directory. It also generates screen shots for your tests if you enable it, and error shots for your failed test cases as well.

Start at the report index.
